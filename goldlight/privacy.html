等光 Goldlight 隱私權政策（Privacy Policy）

生效日期：2026-11-26

中文

等光 沒有帳號、沒有廣告、不追蹤，也沒有自己的伺服器。開發者拿不到你的任何資料。

位置

  • 只在「使用 App 期間」取用，用來計算你所在地的日出日落時間與方位，並取得當地天氣。日月位置在手機上計算。
  • 座標會傳給 Apple 的天氣服務（WeatherKit）與地名查詢服務，換取預報與地名。天氣服務的座標不會附上你的身分。開發者收不到，也不保存。
  • 不想提供位置也能用：改選景點，或在地圖上長按選一個地點。
  • 打開通知後，App 會在背景更新你收藏地點與上次位置的天氣，不會在背景取得你的新位置。

收藏地點、拍攝回報與照片

  • 只存在你的手機。回報時附上的照片只複製到 App 裡，不會上傳。
  • iPhone 的 iCloud 備份或電腦備份可能會一併備份這些資料，那是由 Apple 處理。
  • 匯出拍攝回報（CSV）只有你自己按的時候才會產生檔案，存到哪裡由你決定。

訂閱與購買

  • 由 Apple 處理；開發者看不到你的付款資料、帳號或聯絡方式。App 只在手機上記錄「是否為 Pro」。

第三方

  • 天氣資料由 Apple Weather 提供，Apple 的處理方式見 Apple 隱私權政策。
  • 沒有分析工具、沒有廣告 SDK，也沒有其他第三方服務。

刪除你的資料

  • 收藏與回報可以在 App 裡逐筆刪除；刪除 App 會一併刪除 App 裡的所有資料。

兒童

  • App 不蒐集任何人的資料，包括兒童。

政策變更

  • 若有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期。

English

Effective date: 2026-11-26

Goldlight has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no servers of its own. The developer receives none of your data.

Location

  • Used only while you use the app, to work out sun and moon times and directions (on your phone) and to get the local weather.
  • Coordinates are sent to Apple's weather (WeatherKit) and geocoding services in exchange for the forecast and the place name. Weather requests do not carry your identity. The developer never receives or stores them.
  • You can decline and pick a spot or drop a pin instead.
  • With alerts on, the app refreshes the weather of your saved places and last location in the background. It does not get a new location in the background.

Saved places, reports and photos

  • They stay on your phone. A photo added to a report is copied into the app and never uploaded.
  • iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone may include them; that is handled by Apple.
  • CSV export happens only when you ask for it, and you choose where the file goes.

Subscriptions and purchases

  • Handled by Apple; the developer never sees your payment details. The app only remembers whether you have Pro.

Third parties

  • Weather data is provided by Apple Weather, see the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • No analytics, no ad SDK, no other third-party service.

Deleting your data

  • Delete saved places and reports one by one in the app; deleting the app removes everything in it.

Children

  • The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

  • Any change is published on this page together with a new effective date.
